Witman Daniel Saldaña Vargas

Game Developer • Roblox Systems & Gameplay Programmer (Lua / Luau)

I am a gameplay and systems programmer specializing in Roblox Studio and Lua / Luau. I have deep knowledge in designing and building scalable game systems: secure client-server architectures, low-latency network replication, match state management, and reliable data persistence.

I specialize in developing asymmetric multiplayer games (1vs9 formats with differentiated roles), having engineered complete mechanics for hunter tracking, survivor stealth, active/passive abilities, interactive skill-check minigames, and real-time network synchronization.
